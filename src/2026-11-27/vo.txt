Danny's fixed cars on this street for fifteen years. Best mechanic for miles. But his Google listing had a problem. The photos? Not his garage. They were the place across the road. So people searched Danny's... and pulled into the wrong yard. Even Mrs Patel. Twelve years a regular. Booked her MOT. Over there. And then it got worse. The other guy walked over. To say thanks... for all the business. That night, Danny sorted it. Real photos. His own website. A proper booking button. By Monday, the queue was in his yard. And all he did was search two words.
