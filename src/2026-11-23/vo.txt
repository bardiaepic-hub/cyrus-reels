He had his card out. Ready to pay. Then he gave up.

This is Jay. It's his mum's birthday tomorrow, and he wants a cake from Crumb and Co. Best bakery he knows.

He searches them. Taps the website. And waits. And waits.

It finally loads. It's tiny. He pinches, zooms, scrolls. The menu's a PDF.

He finds the cake. No price. Just "call to order".

So he calls. It rings out. They shut at five.

Fine. There's a contact form. Seven boxes. He fills in every single one. Hits send.

Error. Phone number not valid. And the whole form's empty again.

Four minutes in. Card still in his hand.

He goes back to Google. Second result. A bakery he's never even heard of.

Order a cake. Pick a size. Pay. Done. Forty seconds.

Next morning, Crumb and Co open up. No orders. They call it a quiet week.

It wasn't quiet. Jay just couldn't get through.
